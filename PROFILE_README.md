# Vaibhav Gupta

Product manager building trusted data, analytics, and workflow platforms — now focused on evidence-grounded AI workflows, agents, and decision-support products.

I started as a software engineer and now lead product for complex data and AI platforms: enterprise data catalogs, AI-enabled workflow tools, and cybersecurity SaaS. The common thread is turning messy, high-stakes information into something people and systems can actually trust and act on.

**Flagship project:** [Resolution Intelligence](https://github.com/guptvaibhav/ai-product-portfolio/tree/main/projects/resolution-intelligence) — an evidence-grounded support copilot concept for Tier-1 engineers, built as a team capstone. Retrieval before synthesis, cited recommendations, confidence-based human routing.

**What I work on:**

- Trusted data foundations — catalog, metadata, and data quality product ownership
- AI-enabled decision workflows and agentic copilots, grounded in evidence rather than free-form generation
- Analytics and KPI standardization at enterprise scale
- Guardrails, evaluation, and human-in-the-loop design for AI features
